# SC County Health Dashboard

A two-page Power BI dashboard exploring which county-level conditions are most associated with differences in life expectancy across South Carolina's 46 counties.

## The Question

Which community conditions — economic, behavioral, access, and educational — are most strongly associated with differences in life expectancy across South Carolina counties?

## Data & Methodology

- **Source:** County Health Rankings & Roadmaps, 2025 South Carolina county data
- **Tools:** Data prepared in Power Query; dashboard built in Power BI
- **Note:** Each point represents one county. This dashboard explores associations, not causation — county-level patterns may not reflect individual-level outcomes, and findings are limited by the number of counties analyzed.

## Key Findings

- **Child poverty** has one of the clearest relationships with life expectancy in the dataset — a strong downward trend across counties.
- **Adult smoking rate** also shows a clear downward relationship with life expectancy, with some exceptions.
- **High school graduation rate** has a weaker, more variable positive relationship with life expectancy.
- **Rurality is linked to lower primary care physician rates** — a possible pathway connecting rural counties to worse health outcomes, rather than a direct effect of rurality itself.
- **Uninsured adult rate** has the weakest relationship with life expectancy of the five factors examined.
